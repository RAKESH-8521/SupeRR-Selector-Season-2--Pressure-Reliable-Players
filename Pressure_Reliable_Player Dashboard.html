    Method in four steps
    1. Measure pressure. A smooth win-probability model (chase AUC 0.89) simulates eight outcomes per ball to get its leverage.
    2. Value over expected. Runs and wickets against the average player in the same state, role and format.
    3. Shrink the noise. Usual skill and the pressure effect are estimated separately, so small samples cannot fool the ranking.
    4. Test it. Match-level bootstrap, split-half checks and four alternative pressure definitions.

    Strike rate and economy are descriptive and not adjusted for situation. The ranking uses value over expected. Opposition, venue and pitch are not adjusted for.
